# Implant crowns and bridges (screw-retained on Ti-bases)

Status: **in progress** (2026-10-25). Phase A has landed: library format, synthetic library, scan-body registration and the Ti-base fitting surface. Phase B (emergence, screw channels, implant bridges, schemas, UI, runner case) is next.
Depends on:
- [2026-10-25-bridge-mode.md](2026-10-25-bridge-mode.md) (pontics, connectors, union, anterior teeth)
- [2026-10-25-framework-bridges.md](2026-10-25-framework-bridges.md) (the titanium and CoCr materials)

## Goal

Design **screw-retained** implant restorations on Ti-bases, as exocad's Implant module does:
- single implant crowns, and implant units in bridges
- implant positions found by matching a library **scan body** to the scanned one
- the restoration bonded to a library **Ti-base**, with an emergence profile through the soft tissue and a **screw
  channel**, straight or angled

The plan's MVP listed implants under Out; this spec brings in the screw-retained Ti-base workflow.

### What is different from a crown on a natural prep

| | Natural prep | Implant on a Ti-base |
|---|---|---|
| Where the unit sits | Margin traced on the scan | **Scan body** in the scan, matched to a library model, gives the implant pose |
| Fitting surface | Prep scan plus cement gap | **Library Ti-base** plus bonding gap (machined geometry, not a scan) |
| Below the gingiva | Nothing (the margin is the edge) | **Emergence profile** from the Ti-base shoulder up through the soft tissue |
| Retention | Cement | **Screw** through a channel in the restoration |
| Bridge path | Common axis over the preps | Common path over the Ti-bases. Divergent implants are limited by the Ti-base geometry |

### Clinical and technical facts (sources at the end)

- exocad's Implant module designs screw-retained crowns and bridges and custom abutments, and supports angled screw
  channels.
- Ti-base libraries come per implant system and platform, with **engaging** scan bodies (anti-rotation: single
  units) and **non-engaging** ones (bridges).
- Angled screw channels (ASC) allow the access hole up to about **25°** from the implant axis; some systems allow
  28°.

## Decisions (please confirm)

1. **Restoration type:** `implant-crown`, screw-retained on a Ti-base. Its role is abutment.
   - A single unit uses an engaging Ti-base; a bridge unit uses a non-engaging one. This is enforced, and it matches
     clinical practice: engaging bases cannot seat a bridge along one path.
   - Materials: zirconia, lithium disilicate (single units), PMMA (temporary), titanium and CoCr.
2. **Implant libraries:**
   - An **own, documented format**: JSON with metadata plus STL meshes for the scan body, the Ti-base and the screw.
     Libraries live in `data/implant-libraries/`, which is gitignored like all data.
   - The repo ships **one synthetic generic library** generated by `tools/fixtures` (a cylinder Ti-base with a
     shoulder and a scan body with a flat). No vendor geometry goes into git.
   - **Real vendor libraries need the vendor's permission**, and importing exocad-format libraries is out until that
     is settled. **This needs a decision from you** (who to ask, and which systems first).
3. **Defaults** (placeholders, to confirm in domain QA):
   - Ti-base bonding gap 0.03 mm
   - screw channel diameter from the library (synthetic: 2.2 mm)
   - minimum wall around the channel 0.8 mm
   - straight channel unless the exit is buccal/labial or incisal; ASC up to the library's maximum (synthetic: 25°)
   - the largest divergence between implants in one bridge comes from the library (synthetic: 20°)
4. **The emergence profile is designed, not scanned.**
   - It is a loft from the Ti-base shoulder to the crown's cervical contour at the gingival opening around the scan
     body.
   - A gingiva scan (emergence profile scan) is a follow-up: it needs a new scan role.
5. **Step mapping (keeps saved documents loading):**
   - For implant units, the `margin` step is labelled **"Implant position"** and holds the scan-body match.
   - The `axis` step holds the insertion path: common in bridges, the implant axis for single units.
   - No new `StepId`.

## Scope

In:
- `implant-crown` units, single and in bridges.
- The library format, the loader, and the synthetic generic library.
- **Scan-body registration:** a click, an initial pose, then ICP. The fit quality is reported.
- Fitting surface on the Ti-base (the existing `buildIntaglio`: a Ti-base is star-shaped).
- Emergence profile loft.
- Screw channel, straight or angled, by boolean difference, with exit and wall checks.
- Engaging rotation from the scan body's flat (single units).
- Common path and divergence check for implant bridges.
- Pontics and connectors as in bridge mode; mixed bridges (implant and natural abutments) are refused (decision
  below).
- Export: the restoration STL plus a sidecar with the implant poses, library ids and channel axes, for CAM.
- Synthetic golden case `35i–[36]–37i`.

Out:
- Custom abutments: a two-piece design with a cemented crown, which exocad also does. It would be its own spec.
- Multi-unit abutments (MUA) and bars.
- Full-arch hybrid prostheses with pink gingiva (exocad's FP3 workflow).
- Implant planning and surgical guides.
- Gingiva or emergence scans.
- Importing vendor libraries.
- **Mixed implant and natural-tooth bridges.** They are rigidly connected bridges on supports that move differently
  (osseointegrated versus periodontal ligament). This is clinically contentious, so it is refused for now. exocad
  does not stop it; tell me if you want it allowed with a warning.

## Design

### Document and commands

- `RestorationType` adds `implant-crown`.
- `ToothDesign.implant?: { libraryId; scanBodyPose: Pose; engaging: boolean; fit: { rmsMm; maxMm; inlierFraction }; source }`.
- `ToothDesign.screwChannel?: { angleDeg; azimuthDeg }`: 0° is straight, along the implant axis.
- `DesignParams` gains `tiBaseGapMm?`, `channelWallMm?` and `emergenceShape?: 'straight' | 'concave'` (all optional,
  with defaults).
- New commands:
  - `SetImplant { fdi, implant }` (document command): marks the implant-position step done and the rest stale.
  - `SetScrewChannel { fdi, angleDeg, azimuthDeg }` (clinical command, so also an agent tool): marks `proposal`
    stale.
- **Reducer:**
  - The bridge common path is over the implant axes of the implant units. `setAxis` on one sets it for all, as in
    bridge mode.
  - `setCaseSetup` refuses a bridge that mixes `implant-crown` with natural-tooth abutments (decision above).

### Library (`apps/web/src/geometry/implantLibrary.ts`)

- `ImplantLibraryEntry`:
  - ids: `id`, `system`, `platform`
  - meshes: `scanBody` (closed), `tiBase` (closed), and the `shoulderLoop` (the Ti-base's restoration margin ring)
  - its frame: the platform origin at 0, the implant axis +z, and the anti-rotation flat along +x
  - fixed values: `channelDiameterMm`, `ascMaxDeg`, `maxDivergenceDeg`, `engaging`, `bondingGapMm`
- Validated with zod. Meshes are checked closed on load.
- `tools/fixtures/generate.mts` writes `data/implant-libraries/synthetic-generic@1/`.

### Geometry

- **Scan-body registration** (`scanBody.ts`):
  - The user clicks the scan body top.
  - An initial axis comes from a local PCA of the scan points within the scan body radius; the cylinder's long
    axis is the implant axis.
  - The initial position is where that axis meets the scan body top, and the rotation is a coarse search over
    12 angles.
  - Then point-to-plane ICP of the library scan body onto the scan points within a 6 mm radius, with outliers
    beyond 0.3 mm rejected.
  - Output: the pose, RMS, max and inlier fraction.
  - Gate: RMS ≤ 0.05 mm, inlier fraction ≥ 0.9.
- **Fitting surface:** the Ti-base mesh at the implant pose is offset by `tiBaseGapMm`. Then the existing
  `buildIntaglio` runs with that offset surface's ray caster, the shoulder loop as the rim and the implant axis.
  This is the same code path as a crown, with the Ti-base in place of the prep.
- **Emergence and anatomy:**
  - **Gingival opening:** the loop where the jaw scan meets the scan body, from the scan points within 0.3 mm of
    the registered scan body's surface (their outer boundary).
  - **Anatomy:** the library tooth is posed as for crowns, with its cervical ring placed on the gingival opening.
    MD comes from the neighbours or connectors, height from the antagonist.
  - **Emergence:** each template column is extended below its cervical ring with a Hermite curve down to the
    matching shoulder-loop point (paired by arc length, as `designCrown` pairs margin and ring). The tangent is
    continuous with the template wall at the top. `emergenceShape` sets the curve's concavity.
  - The rest is the existing pipeline: RBF fit (the shoulder ring is pinned), thickness enforcement, adaptation and
    the band to the fitting surface.
- **Screw channel:**
  - A cylinder of `channelDiameterMm` runs from the Ti-base top along the implant axis, or along the angled axis
    when `angleDeg > 0` (up to `ascMaxDeg`), through the outer surface.
  - It is subtracted with manifold-3d.
  - **Exit check:** the exit centre must lie on an occlusal or lingual-facing surface: n·occlusal > 0.5 for
    posterior teeth, n·(−buccal) > 0.3 for anterior teeth.
  - **Auto-angle:** if a straight channel exits buccally or incisally, the proposer searches the ASC cone (up to
    `ascMaxDeg`) for the smallest angle with a valid exit. If none exists, the gate fails: "screw channel exits
    buccally".
  - **Wall check:** the material around the channel must be ≥ `channelWallMm` (thickness map restricted to
    channel-adjacent vertices).
- **Engaging rotation:** for single units, the scan body's flat sets the Ti-base rotation. This matters only for the
  library's anti-rotation geometry, which is part of the Ti-base mesh.
- **Implant bridges:**
  - The common path is searched like the bridge-mode axis. The undercut faces come from the Ti-base outer surfaces,
    a cone search around the mean implant axis.
  - It fails when the largest divergence between implant axes exceeds `maxDivergenceDeg`.
  - Each unit's channel is cut before the union, and the union is checked as in bridge mode.
- **Checks:**
  - scan-body fit
  - seating on each Ti-base unchanged after the union (the `measureIntaglioGap` method)
  - channel exit and walls
  - divergence
  - closed and printable

### App

- **Setup:** "Implant crown" type, and the library and platform choice per unit.
- **Implant position step:**
  - Click the scan body. The match is shown as a ghost, with the RMS.
  - Accept or re-click, with a manual nudge.
- **Axis step:** the common path for bridges, showing each implant's axis and the divergence.
- **Proposal step:**
  - The anatomy and the emergence are shown.
  - The screw channel is shown as a translucent cylinder, with its angle control (angle and azimuth, limited to the
    library maximum).
- **Adapt step:** occlusion and contacts as for crowns, plus the channel wall map.
- **Export:**
  - The restoration STL with the channels cut.
  - The sidecar, with each unit's library id, implant pose (platform, axis, rotation), channel axis and angle, and
    the fit quality, so CAM and the lab can match Ti-bases.

### AI layer

- **Proposers:**
  - `classical.scan-body-match@1` (from a click, gated on the fit)
  - `classical.screw-channel@1` (auto-angle)
  - the bridge-mode proposers for path, pontics and adaptation
- **Auto-design for implant units:** needs one click per scan body (the frame-click equivalent); then it runs the
  match → path → proposal → channel → adapt → union gate.
- The agent gets `set_screw_channel`. The prompt explains implant terms and that the channel cannot exceed the
  library maximum.

### Synthetic data and pipeline runner

- `syntheticArch` with `missing: [35, 36, 37]`, plus `scanBodies: [{ fdi, pose }]`:
  - The synthetic scan body mesh is merged into the jaw at known poses on the ridge.
  - Example: 35 tilted 5° mesially and 37 tilted 4° distally, so 9° of divergence.
  - The scan body is partly sunk into the gum, like a real scan.
- Golden case `synthetic-implant-bridge-35-37`, with the known poses as the reference.
- Runner metrics:
  - registration error versus the truth (position and angle)
  - fit RMS
  - divergence
  - channel exits, angles and minimum walls
  - seating on each Ti-base
  - union validation
  - `bridge.stl`

## Risks

- **Vendor libraries and licensing.** Without real libraries this is a demo on synthetic geometry; real Ti-base
  tolerances are unknown until then. The decision is listed above.
- **Registration on real scan bodies.** Real scan bodies are partly hidden by soft tissue and scanned with noise.
  ICP needs a good start; the click plus PCA should provide one, and the fit gate catches failures.
- **Emergence profile without a soft-tissue scan.** A designed loft can press on or leave gaps against real tissue.
  A gingiva-scan role is the follow-up.
- **Screw-channel booleans** near thin walls can leave slivers. The wall check and the printability check catch
  them.

## Implementation summary

### Phase A (2026-10-25): library format, synthetic library, scan-body registration, Ti-base fitting surface
- **`apps/web/src/geometry/implantLibrary.ts`:**
  - The manifest format (`dcad.implant-library`, `library.json` plus STLs), with a fixed frame: platform centre,
    +z axis, +x flat.
  - `makeImplantEntry` validates meshes (closed, oriented, positive volume), the shoulder loop (CCW about +z, on the
    Ti-base) and the scan-body centring.
  - Loaders: `implantLibraryFromBuffers`, `loadImplantLibrary(read)`.
  - Pose helpers: `implantToWorld`, `placeAtImplantPose`, `implantPoseToPose`, `compareImplantPoses`.
  - `implantLibraryNode.ts` (Node directory loader and writer) is kept out of the browser exports.
- **`syntheticImplant.ts`:**
  - `syntheticImplantLibrary()` (`synthetic-generic@1`): engaging and non-engaging Ti-bases, channel Ø2.2 mm,
    ASC 25°, divergence 20°, bonding gap 0.03 mm.
  - `syntheticImplantScan(...)`: scan bodies merged into a synthetic jaw at known poses, with optional noise.
  - `tools/fixtures/implant-library.mts` writes the library under `data/implant-libraries/`; with `--scan` it also
    writes a demo scan with scan bodies.
- **`scanBody.ts` `registerScanBody`:**
  - Click, then local PCA, then the top face, then 12 coarse angles (the best 3 refined), then point-to-plane ICP on
    area-uniform samples.
  - Gate: RMS ≤ 0.05 mm, inliers ≥ 0.9, coverage ≥ 0.6.
- **`tiBase.ts` `tiBaseFittingSurface`:** the Ti-base at the pose, offset by the bonding gap, then `buildIntaglio`
  with the lifted shoulder loop as the rim. `measureTiBaseGap` checks the gap.
- **Verified with scratch scripts:**
  - Registration error against the true poses:
    - no noise: ≤ 0.0003 mm and ≤ 0.004°
    - σ 0.02 mm noise, worst of 48 runs: 0.011 mm, 0.089° axis, 0.21° rotation
    - All runs pass the gate, including clicks 1 mm off-centre.
  - Clicks on teeth or the ridge fail the gate.
  - Ti-base gap error: mean 0.0025 mm, max 0.016 mm. No penetration, including on a 4.5° common path.
  - A Ø2.2 channel cut with `differenceMeshes` leaves one closed component.
- **Deviations:**
  - ICP runs on area-uniform samples, not on vertices.
  - Normal-consistency rejection keeps neighbouring teeth from pulling the fit.
  - The gate gains a coverage criterion.
  - Manifest additions: `ascPivotZMm`, `scanRegionHeightMm`, `notice`.
  - The intaglio rim is the shoulder lifted by the gap.
- **Open:**
  - Vendor Ti-bases with open screw holes need capping, unless the channel is at least as wide as the hole.
  - The gingival-opening loop and the emergence loft are phase B. They need a `crown.ts` hook that passes the
    fitting side in, instead of building it from a prep.
