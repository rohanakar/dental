# One look: landing page, sign-in, editor and 3D view

Status: **done** (2026-10-26).

## Goal

The user (2026-10-26): "Improve the design as well; the overall theme looks different in the landing page and the
actual login screen and the rendering. Address those."

Side-by-side captures (light and dark colour scheme, 1400 × 900) show three breaks between the surfaces:

1. **Light and dark disagree.** The landing page is always light. The sign-in screen, case list and editor follow
   the operating system. On a Mac in dark mode, the visitor goes from a white page to a near-black login.
2. **Two type systems.** The landing page uses Hanken Grotesk with navy ink. Every app screen uses the system font
   with graphite ink. The sign-in card is a generic system-styled box that shares nothing with the page that sent the
   visitor there, except the blue.
3. **The 3D view is not what the landing page shows.** The landing renders were staged by `landing-shots.mjs`: the
   antagonist hidden and "Focus on the prep" off. In the editor, after auto-design the focus view draws the
   neighbours and the antagonist at 40% opacity with no depth writes. Every hidden layer shows through as grey
   streaks. The move handles use three.js's pure red, green and blue, which match nothing else on screen (the frame
   legend uses green for mesial). The landing page's editor captures show the old system-font UI.

## Decision (user, 2026-10-26)

**Light everywhere, dark as a toggle.** The landing page, sign-in, case list and editor all open in the landing
page's light theme. The editor keeps its dark theme behind a toolbar toggle, remembered per browser. The session
screens are always light.

## Scope

**In:**
- **Theme:**
  - the editor's tokens aligned with the landing's (ink, muted ink, hairline, field outline, page ground);
  - Hanken Grotesk in the app;
  - light by default;
  - a dark toggle in the editor.
- **Session screens** (sign-in, create account, case list, new case) in the landing world: wordmark, display
  heading, landing-style fields and buttons. On wide screens the sign-in shows the same framed render as the landing
  hero.
- **Rendering:**
  - see-through scans draw only their front surface (no streaks);
  - the surroundings stay warmer;
  - the move handles use the frame's axis colours;
  - the viewport ground follows the theme.
- **Landing captures** re-shot from the restyled editor (`tools/e2e/landing-shots.mjs`), with updated provenance.
- **DESIGN.md:** one type family, the theme rule, the session screens, the rendering rules.

**Out:**
- A dark landing page (the user chose light everywhere).
- New 3D lighting or materials beyond the focus view and the handles. The matcaps already match the landing renders.
- Layout changes inside the editor panel (done in the new-user journey).

## Design

### Theme (`apps/web/src/styles.css`, `ui/theme.ts`)
- **Tokens.** `:root` holds the light tokens, with `color-scheme: light`. They are aligned with the landing page:
  - text Deep Navy Ink #0f1b2d;
  - muted Muted Slate #5d6878;
  - hairline #e4e8ee for panels, with control outlines on a slightly darker #dde1e6;
  - field outline #8792a2;
  - page ground #f5f7fa;
  - viewport #e9ecf0 (the landing's `--render-bg`).
- **Dark tokens** move from `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` to `:root[data-theme='dark']`.
- **`ui/theme.ts`:**
  - a small store with `'light' | 'dark'`, read from and written to `localStorage` (`dcad.theme`, wrapped in
    try/catch, light when unavailable);
  - `useEditorTheme()` sets `data-theme` on `<html>` while the editor is mounted and removes it on unmount, so the
    session screens are always light.
- **Toggle:** a toolbar icon button, "Dark theme", with `aria-pressed`. It uses authored moon and sun icons.
- **Viewport:** the viewport background follows the store instead of the media query.
- **Type:**
  - one `@font-face` for Hanken Grotesk (the landing's self-hosted variable file), shared by both stylesheets;
  - editor `--font` is Hanken Grotesk, then the system stack;
  - sizes are unchanged (13px body, 12px small, 15px title);
  - measurements keep tabular figures.

### Session screens (`ui/session/*`)
- **Page:**
  - white ground with the landing nav's wordmark "Dental CAD" top left, linking to the landing page;
  - content in a 1120px container;
  - "What is this? See the product page" is replaced by the wordmark link.
- **Sign-in and create account:**
  - two columns on wide screens: the form, and the framed hero render (`landing/assets/hero-crown.webp`, 14px
    corners, the landing's frame shadow) on the render ground;
  - one column below 860px, without the render;
  - heading in the landing's display style ("Sign in to your lab" / "Create your lab account", 28px, weight 650,
    −0.02em);
  - fields 44px tall with 8px corners, as on the landing;
  - the primary button in the landing's style;
  - "No account needed" keeps its two options and their lines.
- **Case list and new case:** the same page, wordmark and heading style. The cases table and the new-case form keep
  their structure, with landing-style fields and buttons.

### Rendering (`engine/Viewer.ts`, `engine/gizmo.ts`, `tools/focusTool.ts`)
- **Front surface only.** A see-through scan (focus surroundings, the antagonist, or an opacity below 1 in the object
  panel) gets a depth-only pre-pass child that shares its geometry. The pre-pass is in the transparent queue
  (`renderOrder` 1, `colorWrite` off, the same polygon offset and sides); the colour pass follows (`renderOrder` 2).
  - Opaque objects (the crown, the prep in focus) are drawn first, so they still show through.
  - Inside a see-through scan, only its nearest surface is blended. No layered walls, no streaks.
- **Surroundings:** desaturation drops from 0.6 to 0.35, so neighbours and gums stay recognisably tooth and gum.
- **Move handles:** the TransformControls materials are recoloured once: X (mesial) in the frame's mesial green,
  Y (buccal) in buccal orange, Z (occlusal) in occlusal blue. The hover highlight stays.
- The viewport ground comes from the theme.

### Landing captures
`landing-shots.mjs` runs against the restyled editor. Every PNG is converted with `cwebp` at the existing sizes and
crops (`-q 82` for renders, lossless for UI captures). Each `.webp.json` gets a new provenance line naming the commit
basis.

## Implementation summary

The landing page, the screens between it and the editor, the editor and its 3D view now read as one product: one
light theme, one typeface, one ink. The 3D view shows what the landing renders show.

**What was built, and where**
- **Theme** (`apps/web/src/styles.css`, `ui/theme.ts`).
  - Hanken Grotesk is shared through an `@font-face` on the landing's self-hosted file.
  - Light tokens use the landing's ink #0f1b2d, Muted Slate #5d6878, field outline #8792a2 and ground #f5f7fa.
  - Dark tokens moved to `:root[data-theme='dark']`.
  - `useTheme` and `setTheme` store the choice in `localStorage` (`dcad.theme`, in try/catch, light by default).
  - `useEditorTheme` (the `EditorTheme` component in `App.tsx`) sets `data-theme` only while the editor is mounted.
  - The toolbar's "Dark theme" toggle (`Toolbar.tsx`) uses authored moon and sun icons (`icons.tsx`).
  - The viewport ground follows the store (`Viewport.tsx`).
- **Session screens** (`ui/session/SessionPage.tsx` (new), `AuthForm.tsx`, `CaseList.tsx`, `SessionGate.tsx`,
  `session.css`, `journey.css`).
  - The landing bar and wordmark frame every screen.
  - Sign-in and create account: "Sign in to your lab" and "Create your lab account" beside the framed hero render
    with a caption. A single column below 860px.
  - The case list: a "Cases" heading, the new case on a tinted panel, then the table at 15px.
  - Fields are 44px. The primary button is 44px; secondaries are 40px, white with the field outline, as on the
    landing page.
- **Rendering** (`engine/Viewer.ts`, `engine/axisColors.ts` (new), `engine/gizmo.ts`, `tools/focusTool.ts`,
  `tools/controller.ts`).
  - A see-through scan gets a depth-only child (`syncPrepass`).
  - `orderSeeThrough` draws see-through objects back to front, each as pre-pass then colour, before every render and
    screenshot.
  - The focus view greys the surroundings by 0.35 (was 0.6).
  - The frame palette moved to `AXIS_COLORS`, and the move handles use it through `TransformControls.setColors`
    (hover in Draft Amber).
- **Landing captures.** `tools/e2e/landing-shots.mjs` now does three things differently:
  - it opens "Show the checks" for the check-list capture (cropped to the first two steps, clear of the footer);
  - it scrolls the panel to the top;
  - it keeps the toolbar in the Finalize capture.

  Four files were re-shot: `auto-design` (626 × 570), `draft-banner` (630 × 272), `editor-finalize` (2000 × 1250)
  and `editor-panel` (680 × 1420). They were converted with `cwebp`, and `index.html` has the new sizes and alt text.
  Each `.webp.json` names the commit basis.
- **DESIGN.md:**
  - the overview: one look, with the session screens in the landing world;
  - editor tokens and their names (Deep Navy Ink, Muted Slate), the theme rule, the font;
  - the session screens' layout;
  - a new 3D View section;
  - the move handles under The Data Is Not Decoration Rule.

**Deviations from the spec**
- **Draw order:** see-through objects are drawn back to front, each with its own pre-pass, not "every pre-pass, then
  every colour pass". The first version hid the prep's jaw under the see-through antagonist in the occlusal view.
  Drawn back to front, both arches show, layered, and neither has streaks.
- **Hairline:** the editor keeps #dde1e6 for panel edges and control outlines alike. The session screens use the
  landing's #e4e8ee for their own rules. A second editor hairline token was not worth the churn.
- **Captures re-shot:** only the four landing captures that show editor UI. The hero and the two map renders are 3D
  only, with the antagonist hidden and focus off, so they already match.
- **Finalize capture:** it now includes the toolbar, which carries the product name since the name was chosen, so it
  is 2000 × 1250 instead of 2000 × 1194.
- **Sidecar:** `.impeccable/design.json` (the DESIGN.md sidecar written by `/impeccable document`) still lists the
  old editor tokens. Regenerating it is left to that command.
