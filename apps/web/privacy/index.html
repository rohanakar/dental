Dental CAD

Privacy

Dental CAD is a beta. This page says what the service keeps and what you should and should not put in it.

The data rule

What we keep

  • Your account: name, work email and a hashed password.
  • Your lab workspace: its cases, the scan files you upload, the design versions you save, and the STL files you export.
  • Feedback you send from the feedback page: the message, the reply address if you give one, and which page it came from.
  • Basic server logs (time, path, status) for fixing faults.

Where it lives and who can see it

  • On our own server, in a database and a file store. Members of your lab workspace see its cases; other labs cannot.
  • The person running the beta can reach the server and can read it when fixing a fault or answering your feedback.
  • If you use the AI assistant or paste a prescription, the text you send, and the measured numbers it needs, go to the model provider that answers it. Scan files are not sent.
  • The demo case uses synthetic scans and needs no account.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not use your cases to train models.

Deleting your data

Write to and we will delete your account and its cases.

Not a medical device

Dental CAD is not a medical device. A dental professional must check every design before it is made.
