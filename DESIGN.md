# Design System: Dental CAD (working name)

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Verifiable Instrument"**

DentalCAD is a precision instrument that shows its working. It has two surfaces. The landing page (`apps/web/index.html`) persuades a lab technician or clinician on a first visit. The editor at `/app/` is where they then work all day. Both play the category standard straight, at the craft level of exocad, Dentbird and Linear / Vercel: cool white and blue-grey grounds, a single blue, hairlines instead of boxes, and numbers set as numbers. Every claim on screen is something the user can check: a measured value with its unit, a pass mark, a colour map with its scale.

The two surfaces share one look and differ in density. Both are light, set in Hanken Grotesk with deep navy ink and Muted Slate. The landing world has a white ground and display sizes tracked tight. Its sections breathe, and it shows real captures of the editor in a hairline frame with one soft, navy-tinted shadow. The screens between the two (sign-in, create account, the case list) belong to the landing world: its bar and wordmark, its display heading, 44px fields and 8px corners, and the hero render beside the sign-in form. The editor world is dense and flat: instrument sizes (13px body), tonal panels and hairline borders, built for long sessions zoomed into one tooth. Its dark theme is an opt-in from the toolbar (2026-10-26: one look across the surfaces, `specs/2026-10-26-one-look.md`). Both use the same blue (#2f6fde) as their only accent. Both use the same Viewport Grey behind the 3D scene, and the same colour-map stops, which the engine owns and every surface copies.

Ownership: the landing sections of this file are recorded from `apps/web/src/landing/landing.css`, and its direction contract is `apps/web/.impeccable/surfaces/index-html.md`. The parts marked "editor" are **maintained by the editor polish work** (`specs/2026-10-25-ux-critique-polish.md`), recorded from `apps/web/src/styles.css` and `apps/web/src/ui/**/*.css`. Editor token changes land there first and are recorded here afterwards. The colour-map stops belong to `apps/web/src/engine/colormap.ts` and `apps/web/src/tools/crownLegend.ts`. The confirmed visual rejection (PRODUCT.md, Brand Commitments) is that marketing surfaces carry real product renders, never illustration, a glowing-tooth hero or stock clinic photography.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One blue accent (#2f6fde) on both surfaces. Everything else is a neutral, a status or data.
- Measurements in tabular figures, always with their unit, and their limit where one exists.
- Hairlines and tone for structure. Shadows appear only on the landing page's framed renders and the overlays that sit on them.
- Colour maps are clinical data, transcribed exactly from the engine, never decoration.
- Real product renders, never illustration, on marketing surfaces.
- Dashed and dotted strokes mean not there yet, or a target.

## Colors

A cool, nearly colourless system: white and blue-grey grounds, navy or graphite ink, one blue, and colour kept for status and clinical data.

### Primary
- **Instrument Blue** (#2f6fde): the one accent on both surfaces. It fills primary buttons and draws the 2px focus ring. It is the hover colour of landing links, the second line of the hero headline, the caret, the range and checkbox accent, and the dashed dot of a draft step. In the editor's dark theme it lifts to Night Blue (#5b8ef0).
- **Deep Instrument Blue** (#1f56b8): blue used as text. On the landing page it colours links at rest (`--accent-ink`). In the editor (`--accent-strong`) it is the text of pressed toggles, the active step, draft badges and link buttons on the tint. Dark: Pale Instrument Blue (#9bbcf7).
- **Hover Blue** (#2560c8): the landing primary button on hover. The editor has no hover token: its primary mixes 14% black into the accent instead.
- **Blue Wash** (landing #e8effc; editor #e6eefb, which resolves `color-mix(in srgb, accent 12%, panel)`; editor dark #242d3e): the ground for "selected, but not the main action". Pressed toggles, the active step, draft badges, hint boxes, the draft banner, the user's own assistant messages, and the landing page's step numbers.

### Status
- **Pass Green** (editor #177245, dark #3cc47c; landing #1d7f4e): pass marks, done dots, the "Ready to mill" heading, and the landing checks count. In the editor, `--ok-text` currently aliases `--ok`.
- **Fail Red** (#b42318, shared; editor dark #ff8a80): errors, failed checks, invalid field borders and fail values in read-outs. The landing page pairs it with Fail Wash (#fdeceb) for its form error box.
- **Stale Amber** (editor only. Icon #a86400, wash #fff6e0, ink #7a5300. Dark: #e0a325, #3a2f12, #f3d48a): out-of-date steps, warnings and notices, "Not ready to mill", the offline badge, and the strip that names a missing step.

### Data
The engine owns these stops, and the 3D surface, the editor legend and the landing scales all draw from them.
- **Clearance maps** (occlusion and proximal; diverging): Penetration Red (#c0392b), Salmon (#f39c6b), Contact Bone (#f4f1ea), Gap Sky (#7fb3e0), Ample Blue (#2e6bb5). The range is −0.2 to 0.5 mm. The stops are re-centred so the neutral bone falls exactly at 0 mm contact, 28.57% along the bar. The 0.05 mm target sits at 35.71%.
- **Thickness map** (sequential, with a threshold): everything below the material minimum is Penetration Red, with a hard edge. Above it the map runs Just-Enough Straw (#f2e394), Mid Teal (#7fd1c4), Ample Blue (#2e6bb5). The range is 0 to 2 mm, and the zirconia 0.5 mm minimum sits at 25%.
- **Undercut and unmillable maps**: Seated Green (#3d9a5b), Lime (#a7c94a), Amber (#f2c94c), Orange (#f08a3c), Blocked Red (#d64545).
- **Tooth-frame axes** (viewport arrows and their swatches in the Tooth frame step): occlusal (#4c8dff), mesial (#2fbf71), buccal (#f0a030), distal (#a970ff).

### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** Instrument Blue is the only hue on any surface that is not a status or data colour. There is no second brand colour, no per-section accent and no coloured icon set.

**The Tint Is Not The Fill Rule.** The solid accent marks the main action: one per step, form or page region. Repeating the same action is fine: the nav and the hero form both create a lab account. Pressed toggles, the active step, the current unit and drafts take the Blue Wash with Deep Instrument Blue text. Outside actions, the solid accent fills only the tooth chart's marks for what is in the case: crown teeth and their connectors.

**The Data Is Not Decoration Rule.** A map colour always means a clinical value. Map colours appear on the scan or crown surface, on the legend and on scales transcribed from it, and nowhere else; the axis colours appear only on the viewport's frame arrows, their swatches and the crown's move handles (mesial-distal green, buccal orange, occlusal blue; a hovered handle turns Draft Amber #ffd166). Penetration Red (#c0392b) is the crown maps' one warning colour.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Hanken Grotesk, variable 100–900, self-hosted from `apps/web/src/landing/fonts/` (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Hanken Grotesk everywhere: the landing page, the session screens and the editor share the one self-hosted variable file (`@font-face` in both `landing/landing.css` and `styles.css`).
**Label/Mono Font:** ui-monospace, SF Mono, Menlo, at 0.9em, for inline file names such as `.dcad.zip` on the landing page.

**Character:** On the landing page, Hanken Grotesk's variable axis is set at in-between weights (480, 520, 560, 600, 620, 630, 640). Hierarchy therefore moves in small steps, and display sizes are tracked tight, so the page reads like a technical product rather than a brochure. The editor sets the same face at instrument sizes, and gets its hierarchy from weight and muted colour rather than size.

### Hierarchy
Landing:
- **Display** (640, clamp(2.5rem, 4.6vw, 4.125rem), 1.02, −0.035em): the hero headline only. It runs to two lines, and the second line is in Instrument Blue.
- **Headline** (630, clamp(1.875rem, 3.2vw, 2.75rem), 1.08, −0.03em): section titles and the closing call to action.
- **Title** (620, 1.125rem): sub-heads within a section (Today, Not yet).
- **Lede** (400, clamp(1.0625rem, 1.3vw, 1.1875rem), 1.6): the hero subhead, at most 34em wide. Section intros are 1.125rem and at most 62ch wide.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.6): paragraphs, at most 36em wide in two-column sections.
- **Small** (400–480, 0.9375rem): nav links, form notes, captions, checks-panel rows and step descriptions.
- **Label** (520, 0.875rem): form labels. A hint in parentheses follows at 400 in Muted Slate.
- **Micro** (400–620, 0.8125rem): table heads, scale labels, the checks panel's case line and step numbers.
- **Figure** (560–600, tabular): measured values in the checks panel and the gate table.
- **Button** (560, 1rem, line-height 1, −0.005em). Emphasis inside text (step names, caption leads) is 600.

Editor (maintained by the editor polish work):
- **Title** (600, 15px `--fs-title`, −0.01em): the step title, the one heading in the side panel. Session-card titles are 18px.
- **Body** (400, 13px `--fs-body`, 1.45): the default for everything, buttons included. Primary buttons, pressed buttons and object names are 500.
- **Label** (600, 12px `--fs-small`): section labels inside a step (h3, legend) and the colour legend's title, in Muted Slate.
- **Small** (400, 12px): field labels (muted), hints, read-outs, legend text and warnings.
- **Micro** (400, 11px `--fs-micro`): badges, step notes, and the tooth chart's jaw labels and key. Tooth numbers in the chart are 12px.
- **Brand** (650, 14px, −0.01em): the working name in the toolbar.

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Measurement Rule.** Every millimetre, count, threshold and step number is set in tabular figures with its unit. The editor sets `tabular-nums` on the body. The landing page sets it on every measured value, and puts the limit beside the value in the muted ink (1.01 mm, min 0.50).

**The Sentence Case Rule.** Headings, labels, buttons and section labels are in sentence case at normal or negative tracking. Hierarchy comes from weight and the muted ink, never from uppercase or letter-spacing.

## Layout

**Landing.** A centred container 1200px wide, with a gutter of clamp(16px, 4vw, 40px) on each side. The nav is a sticky 64px bar, and in-page anchors scroll with 80px of padding.
- **Hero:** two columns (1fr and 1.08fr) with a clamp(40px, 5vw, 72px) gap. The copy and sign-up form sit left. The framed render sits right, and the checks panel overlaps its lower-right edge (24px past the right side, 64px below).
- **Sections:** vertical padding of clamp(80px, 10vw, 136px), alternating White Ground and Cool Mist. Intros are at most 760px wide, with 40–64px below them.
- Workflow is a full-width shot over a three-column numbered list. Checks is the gate table, then the two maps side by side. AI assist runs 1fr and 0.8fr. Scope has two columns. The closing call to action is centred.
- **At 1080px:** the hero goes to one column, the render is capped at 760px, and the steps go to two columns.
- **At 760px:**
  - the nav hides its section links;
  - the hero reorders to headline, lede, render with the checks panel (now in the flow, overlapping by 48px), then the form and the Try the demo button;
  - the render crops to 16:11, and the editor shot swaps to the side panel at 1:1;
  - every grid goes to one column, and table rows stack as name and value above the description.
- **At 420px:** the nav's call to action uses its short label.

**Editor (maintained by the editor polish work).** A full-viewport grid: the toolbar across the top, a 340px side panel on the left, and the viewport filling the rest. On Case setup the panel widens to 420px for the sixteen-tooth chart rows.
- **Side panel:** 12px padding and 14px between sections. Step bodies stack at 12px. Controls are 28px tall, 8px apart in rows and 4px apart in grids. The Back / Next footer sticks to the bottom of the panel.
- **Status line:** the busy indicator and the latest tool message sit at the top of the sticky footer, above Back / Next, so feedback is always in view. A message is 12px on a 6px-cornered ground: Control Grey for a notice, Amber Wash for a warning, a 10% Fail Red wash with Fail Red text for an error. It has a 24px dismiss button, and a new message replaces the old one. A notice leaves with its step; warnings and errors stay until dismissed.
- **Viewport overlays:** the colour legend floats top-right (12px inset, 240px wide). The help sheet (the `?` key or Help in the toolbar) floats top-left, 400px wide, on Panel White with a hairline and 8px corners: shortcuts, then a short glossary. It is not modal. The hint line runs along the bottom left, on a 94% Panel White ground (as the legend) with 6px corners so it stays legible over the scan.
- **Below 720px:** the viewport (45% of the height) stacks above the panel.
- **Session screens:** the landing bar (64px, wordmark left, the screen's own controls right) over a centred container, 1120px wide (960px for the case list), with clamp(32px, 6vw, 72px) above. ## Elevation & Depth

The two surfaces differ on purpose. The landing page lifts its product captures and the overlays on them with a navy-tinted shadow family. Everything else on it is flat and separated by hairlines. The editor is flat throughout: depth comes from tone (Bench Grey under Panel White, with Control Grey for controls) and 1px hairlines. The session screens borrow the landing's frame shadow for the hero render beside the sign-in form, and nothing else.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Frame** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgb(15 27 45 / 0.07), 0 1px 2px rgb(15 27 45 / 0.06), 0 18px 40px -18px rgb(15 27 45 / 0.28)`): framed product shots and the gate table. The 1px ring is the hairline edge, so the frame has no separate border.
- **Overlay** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgb(15 27 45 / 0.08), 0 24px 48px -20px rgb(15 27 45 / 0.35)`): the checks panel where it overlaps the hero render.
- **Inset card** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgb(15 27 45 / 0.08), 0 12px 28px -12px rgb(15 27 45 / 0.3)`): the draft-review crop that sits inside the hero render.

### Named Rules
**The Navy Shadow Rule.** Landing shadows are tinted with Deep Navy Ink (rgb 15 27 45), never black. They fall straight down with a negative spread and are never offset sideways.

**The Flat Instrument Rule.** The editor has no drop shadows. Panels sit on the bench by tone and hairline. The legend floats over the viewport on a 94% Panel White with a hairline. The tooth chart's 2px inset ring marks the active tooth; it is not elevation.

## Shapes

Radius grows with the object, and data stays nearly square.
- **Landing:**
  - 8px for controls: buttons, inputs and the error box;
  - 12px for the checks panel and 14px for framed renders and the gate table;
  - 6px for the skip link, and 4px for focus-ring corners;
  - 3px for scale bars;
  - full circles for step numbers and pass ticks.
- **Editor:**
  - 6px for controls (`--radius-sm`): buttons, fields, step rows, warnings and hint boxes. Segmented groups use 5px inner corners.
  - 8px for containers (`--radius`): cards, the legend, the ready-to-mill list, the dropzone and object cards.
  - 6px for tooth-chart keys too, 4px for the corners of connector brackets, and 3px for the legend bar and chart swatches;
  - 999px pills for badges;
  - circles for status dots.

Step status is drawn as a shape, with screen-reader text alongside: a ring is not done, a filled green dot is done, a half-filled amber dot is out of date, and a dashed blue ring is in an open draft.

**The Dashed Means Not-There Rule.** Dashed and dotted strokes mark what is not there yet, or not there at all: the dropzone and the drop-to-import overlay, a draft step's dot, a check not yet measured, the first-run empty case list, a pontic (a missing tooth being replaced), an unavailable connector, and the dotted target tick on a map scale. Solid strokes mean measured or committed, such as the solid tick at contact or at the minimum.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use Instrument Blue (#2f6fde) as the only accent, with Deep Instrument Blue (#1f56b8) where blue is text on a tint.
- **Do** set every measurement in tabular figures with its unit, and its limit beside it where one exists (1.01 mm, min 0.50).
- **Do** show the product through real captures of the editor on the synthetic case, framed on Viewport Grey (#e9ecf0) with the Frame shadow, each with its provenance sidecar.
- **Do** copy the colour-map stops, their re-centred positions and the solid and dotted ticks from `colormap.ts` and `crownLegend.ts` whenever a map or scale appears.
- **Do** give each step, form or page region one solid-accent action; pressed, active and draft states take the Blue Wash.
- **Do** separate with hairlines (#e4e8ee on the landing page, #dde1e6 in the editor) before reaching for a box or a shadow.
- **Do** draw icons as authored SVG in `currentColor`, with the control naming the action.
- **Do** build new editor screens from the editor's tokens (`--fs-*`, `--radius-sm`, `--radius`, `--control-h`, `--field-border`, `--field-bg`, `--accent-strong`, `--accent-tint`, `--focus-ring`, `--ok`, `--warn-*`), and give every new colour its dark value alongside the light one.
- **Do** honour reduced motion with the final state: no count-in, no smooth scroll, and 0ms transitions on the landing page.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a second accent hue, or use a map colour (Penetration Red, Straw, Teal, map Amber) as a UI or brand colour.
- **Don't** put illustration, stock clinic photography or a glowing-tooth hero on a marketing surface.
- **Don't** set labels in uppercase with wide tracking, or add eyebrow or kicker labels above headings; hierarchy comes from weight and the muted ink.
- **Don't** use Unicode glyphs (✓ ✗ ⚠ ↶) as icons or status marks.
- **Don't** mark a callout, card or row with a coloured side stripe; a callout is a tinted ground, with at most a hairline all round.
- **Don't** use gradient text, or blended gradients on buttons, panels or section grounds; the only blended gradient is a colour-map scale.
- **Don't** give editor surfaces drop shadows, and don't use black or sideways-offset shadows on the landing page.
- **Don't** use dashed or dotted strokes as decoration; they mean not there yet, or a target.
